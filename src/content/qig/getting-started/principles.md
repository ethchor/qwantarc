## 1. Task first

A screen leads with the job it exists for. Secondary controls move into menus, inspectors and sheets, so the main task is never crowded out.

## 2. People stay in control

Guidance can be skipped, destructive work is confirmed or can be undone, and nothing acts without being asked.

## 3. Clarity over decoration

Legible type, purposeful colour and room to breathe. Content comes before chrome, and every visual element earns its place.

## 4. One vocabulary

The same job uses the same control, in the same place, with the same words, everywhere in a product and across products.

## 5. Depth with purpose

Content sits on opaque surfaces. Controls and navigation float above it on Glass, so people always know what they can act on and what they are reading.

## 6. Adapts to people and places

Light and dark, text size, pointer or touch, keyboard-only use and accessibility settings are first-class, not afterthoughts.

## 7. Fast where work is frequent

Actions people repeat all day happen instantly, without animation, with the cursor where they need it and a shortcut for the command.

## 8. Trustworthy with data

Say what will happen before it happens, and make the consequences for money, records and people explicit.

## 9. Crafted details

Shared design values only, concentric corners, aligned figures, consistent motion, and real empty, loading and error states.
