<h2 id="r1">R1. Glass only in the navigation and control layer</h2>

Sidebars, toolbars, tab bars, popovers, menus, pop-up lists and floating control bars use Glass. Content such as tables, forms, cards and documents stays opaque. A sheet is an opaque card over a dimmed, lightly blurred backdrop.

<h2 id="r2">R2. Never Glass on Glass</h2>

Controls inside a Glass bar are plain. Glass containers are never nested.

<h2 id="r3">R3. Monochrome toolbar symbols</h2>

Toolbar and navigation symbols use the label colour. Colour signals state, never decoration.

<h2 id="r4">R4. One prominent action per view</h2>

The prominent button style appears once in a view. Every other action uses the tinted, normal or plain style.

<h2 id="r5">R5. Destructive is red and confirmed</h2>

Destructive actions use the destructive style and ask for confirmation, or, for frequent and reversible edits, act at once and offer Undo.

<h2 id="r6">R6. Concentric corners</h2>

A rounded shape inside another uses the outer radius minus the padding between them, so the curves run parallel.

<h2 id="r7">R7. Outlined icons</h2>

Icons are outlined line symbols sized to the text beside them. A filled symbol shows only a selected state.

<h2 id="r8">R8. Large titles for hubs</h2>

Hubs, the screens that lead into an area of a product, open with a large title. Working screens use the regular page title row.

<h2 id="r9">R9. Segmented controls are never empty</h2>

A segmented control with single selection always keeps one segment selected.

<h2 id="r10">R10. No popover on a popover</h2>

Close the first popover before opening anything else, then open a sheet or navigate.

<h2 id="r11">R11. Every effect has a fallback</h2>

Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast swap in plain versions of every animation, material and subtle colour.

<h2 id="r12">R12. No floating action buttons</h2>

Global actions live in the toolbar and page actions in the title row, never in a button floating over content.

<h2 id="r13">R13. Alerts are rare</h2>

Use alerts only for critical, actionable moments, with at most three buttons that have specific titles. Never "OK" on its own.

<h2 id="r14">R14. Hit targets</h2>

Every control is at least 44 × 44 points on touch screens and at least 28 × 28 points with a pointer.

<h2 id="r15">R15. Text conventions</h2>

Title Case on buttons and menu items, an ellipsis (…) when a control opens another view, and tabular figures for money and quantities.

<h2 id="r16">R16. Design values only</h2>

Colours, type sizes, radii, shadows and durations come from the system’s named [design values](/design/qig/design-values), never from one-off values.

<h2 id="r17">R17. Real states</h2>

Every list and screen has designed empty, loading and error states.

<h2 id="r18">R18. Keyboard parity</h2>

Everything works from the keyboard with a visible focus ring, and focus is never stolen or lost while people work.
