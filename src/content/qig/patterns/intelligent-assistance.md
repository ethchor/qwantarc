## Best practices

- **Be clear about the source.** Say when something is generated or suggested by AI.
- **People stay in control.** Suggestions are shown for review and are never applied silently.
- **Scope it to the task.** Offer assistance where the work happens, with a way to retry, edit or undo, and progress described in specific words.
- **Keep data private.** Prefer on-device models, and say when data leaves the device.
- **Always offer the manual way** to do the same thing.
- **Admit uncertainty.** When a suggestion may be wrong, say so, and make checking it easy.
