## Best practices

- **Empty is a state, not an absence.** A quiet symbol, a title that names the thing (“No saved drafts”), one sentence on what goes here, and the one action that fills it. See [Empty states](/design/qig/empty-states).
- **Teach by doing.** The empty state is the tutorial, not a separate set of screens to tap through.
- **Tips are optional,** short (two sentences at most), shown in context, one at a time, and never block the screen.
- **Help stays reachable later.** Guided tours can be skipped and replayed.
- **Ask for as little as possible up front.** Defer setup until the moment a feature needs it.
