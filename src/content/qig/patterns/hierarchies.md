## Best practices

- **Columns for working inside a hierarchy.** Each level is a grouped list, the selection is tinted, a trailing chevron leads to the next column, and a row’s actions are quiet icon buttons.
- **A graph for seeing the whole.** Opaque cards on a dotted canvas, links that meet card edges, a Glass zoom toolbar, and actions that appear on hover or focus.
- Keep the path visible with a [breadcrumb](/design/qig/breadcrumbs) when the hierarchy is deep.
