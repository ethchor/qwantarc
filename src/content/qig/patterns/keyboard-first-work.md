## When to use

Work people repeat many times an hour: entering lines into a list, processing a queue, checking items off.

## Best practices

- **The entry field is always ready.** After every action, focus returns to it ([R18](/design/qig/rules#r18)).
- **No animation** on adding, changing or removing lines. Quantities change with steppers or typing.
- **The total is the largest text on screen,** in tabular figures, on a Glass bar with one tinted action.
- **Shortcuts** for the commands people repeat all day, shown on screen beside the control. Standard shortcuts are never overridden.
- **Never move focus or swallow a keystroke** without the person’s action.
- **Forgive mistakes fast:** removing the last line is one key away and can be undone.
