## Best practices

- Open with a large title ([R8](/design/qig/rules#r8)) that names the area.
- Lay out the area’s screens as an even grid of cards, each with an illustration in the area’s colour, a title and one line on what it is for.
- Order cards by how often people need them, not alphabetically.
- An area keeps the same colour everywhere it appears: its hub, its cards and its illustrations.
- A hub never does work itself. Working screens use the regular [page anatomy](/design/qig/page-anatomy).
