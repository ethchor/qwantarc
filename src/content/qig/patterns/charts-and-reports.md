## Best practices

- **Lead with the answer.** Put totals and comparisons above the chart, in words and numbers.
- **Pick the simplest chart** that answers the question: bars for comparison, lines for trends.
- **Label axes and series in text.** Use the system colours in a fixed order, and keep each thing’s colour the same across a report.
- **Accessible by default.** Every chart has a short text summary and the data in a table nearby.
- **Use stat tiles** for two to six key numbers at the top of a report.
