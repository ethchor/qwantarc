## Best practices

- One [search field](/design/qig/search-fields) per screen, at the start of the toolbar row, filtering as people type.
- The placeholder names what is searched: “Search name, email or code”.
- Product-wide search is the [command palette](/design/qig/command-palette), opened with ⌘K or Ctrl+K: places, actions and help in one list.
- With no results, say what was searched, suggest a fix such as checking spelling or clearing filters, and offer to clear.
- Searches stay on their screen. Each screen keeps its own query while it is open.
