## Best practices

- Use a [sheet](/design/qig/sheets) only when the task is self-contained and short, and return people to where they were.
- One sheet at a time. A choice inside a sheet uses a popover or a pop-up button, never another sheet. A long side task means the sheet should have been a page.
- Every modal view has an obvious way out: Cancel, the close button and Escape.
- [Popovers](/design/qig/popovers) hold a few related controls or a short explanation. One at a time; never a popover on a popover ([R10](/design/qig/rules#r10)).
- Confirm before discarding edits, never before closing an untouched sheet.
