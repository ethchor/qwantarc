## Icons

- **One family** of outlined line icons with a consistent 2-point stroke.
- **Size to the text** beside them: 16 points in rows, 18 by default, 20 in toolbars. Don’t scale icons up to fill space.
- **Outlined by default.** A filled variant shows only a selected state ([R7](/design/qig/rules#r7)).
- **Monochrome in toolbars and navigation,** in the label colour ([R3](/design/qig/rules#r3)). Colour belongs to meaning.
- **Name every icon-only control.** It has an accessible label and a tooltip with the same words.
- **Familiar metaphors.** Use the symbol people already know for an action; don’t invent a new one for a common job.

## Illustrations

Each area of a product can have a duotone illustration in its own system colour, used on hubs and dashboards. Keep an area’s colour the same everywhere it appears.

## Images

- Product photos and logos sit in rounded frames with a hairline.
- Never put text over an image without a dimming layer.
- Give every meaningful image alternative text that says what it shows; mark decorative images as decorative.
