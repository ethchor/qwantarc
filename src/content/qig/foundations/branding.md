## Best practices

- **Defer to content.** The logo appears where the context needs it, such as sign-in, the sidebar or a printed document, not on every screen.
- **Draw the logo in the current label colour,** so it follows the appearance. Use the tint only on surfaces that are about the brand.
- **One accent.** The accent is the system blue tint. Don’t introduce screen-specific accent colours.
- **Express personality through words and craft,** not through decoration: clear writing, careful details and consistent behaviour are the brand.
- **Keep the brand out of controls.** Buttons, fields and navigation look the same in every Qwantarc product.
