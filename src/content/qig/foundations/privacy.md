## Best practices

- **Ask in context.** Request a permission, such as the camera or location, only when a feature needs it, and explain why before the system asks.
- **Show only what the task needs.** Mask personal details in lists, such as the middle digits of a phone number, and reveal them where the task needs them.
- **Prefer on-device processing,** especially for intelligent features, and say clearly when data leaves the device.
- **Never put personal data in addresses,** links or anything that might be logged or shared.
- **Make sharing explicit.** Before sending, exporting or syncing personal data, say what goes where.
- **Let people leave.** Exporting and deleting their own data is easy to find and clearly explained.
