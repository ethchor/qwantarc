## When to use

Settings, facts about a record (a label and a value), short pick lists in sheets.

## Best practices

- A row has one primary label; secondary text sits under it or as the trailing value.
- A trailing chevron means the row opens something; a switch means it changes something.

## Avoid

Records with many columns. Use a [table](/design/qig/tables).
