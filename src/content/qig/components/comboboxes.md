## When to use

Choosing from hundreds of records: people, suppliers, customers, products.

## Best practices

- The field shows the chosen record the way people recognise it.
- Offer a clear button when the value is optional.
- Say “No matches” when the filter finds nothing, and offer to create the record when that makes sense.

## Avoid

- Short lists. Use a [pop-up button](/design/qig/pop-up-buttons).
- Free text with suggestions. Use [autocomplete](/design/qig/autocomplete).
