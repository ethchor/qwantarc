## When to use

Quick settings, a filter panel, a short explanation of a value.

## Best practices

- Points at its control. One popover at a time; clicking outside or pressing Escape closes it.
- Changes apply immediately, so there is no Save button.

## Avoid

- Warnings or confirmations. Use an [alert](/design/qig/alerts).
- A popover from a popover ([R10](/design/qig/rules#r10)).
- Long forms. Use a [sheet](/design/qig/sheets).
