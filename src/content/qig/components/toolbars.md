## Best practices

- The leading edge holds Show Sidebar, Home and navigation; the trailing edge holds the account, settings and status.
- Symbols are monochrome ([R3](/design/qig/rules#r3)), and controls inside the bar are plain ([R2](/design/qig/rules#r2)).
- Screens can add their own global actions, but never redraw the bar.

## Avoid

- Page actions. They belong in the title row.
- Floating action buttons ([R12](/design/qig/rules#r12)).
