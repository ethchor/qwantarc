## When to use

- A section of a page: filters, a chart, a block of a form.
- Dashboard tiles and summaries.

## Best practices

A short title, an optional description, and actions in the header or footer.

## Avoid

- Cards inside cards. Use space or a grouped list instead.
- Glass for content ([R1](/design/qig/rules#r1)).
