## When to use

Sections of a record, such as Details, Activity and Settings.

## Best practices

- Each tab’s controls affect only its own panel.
- Titles are short nouns in Title Case.

## Avoid

- Switching a view of the same data. Use a [segmented control](/design/qig/segmented-controls).
- More than six tabs.
