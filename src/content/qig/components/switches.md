## When to use

Settings and preferences.

## Best practices

- Green when on, gray when off; the row’s title says what the switch controls.
- No “On” or “Off” text beside it. The switch shows the state.

## Avoid

Choices that apply only after Save. Use a [checkbox](/design/qig/checkboxes).
