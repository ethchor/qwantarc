## Best practices

- At most two levels, familiar symbols and Title Case labels.
- The current area is highlighted; symbols stay monochrome ([R3](/design/qig/rules#r3)).
- Can be pinned open, or shown as an overlay on narrow windows.

## Avoid

Page actions. They belong in the page’s title row.
