## When to use

- Quick filters in a toolbar, such as “Overdue Only”.
- On and off choices in formatting-style controls.

## Best practices

The title stays the same in both states; the pressed appearance shows the state.

## Avoid

Settings that take effect immediately and persist. Use a [switch](/design/qig/switches).
