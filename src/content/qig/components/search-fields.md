## When to use

- Filtering the list or table on the same screen.
- Finding a record by name or code.

## Best practices

- The placeholder names what can be searched: “Search name, email or code”.
- Filter as people type; Escape clears; show a count of matches when it helps.
- An empty result says what was searched and offers to clear the search.

## Avoid

Searching the whole product. That is the [command palette](/design/qig/command-palette).
