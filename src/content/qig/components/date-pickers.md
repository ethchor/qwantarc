## Best practices

- Show dates in the reader’s locale. In prose, write them out (“2 October 2026”), never as ambiguous numbers.
- Ranges default to something useful, such as today or this month, and say what they cover.
- Limit the range when only some dates make sense, such as no future dates for something that already happened.
- On phones, open the system picker.

## Avoid

Three separate fields for day, month and year.
