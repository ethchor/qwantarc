## When to use

Toolbar actions such as refresh or more, row actions such as edit or details, and the close button of a sheet.

## Best practices

- Every icon button has an accessible label and a tooltip with the same words.
- Three sizes, 28, 36 and 44 points, which grow automatically on touch screens.
- In a row, actions share one style: quiet tertiary symbols with a hover fill.
- “More” (an ellipsis symbol) opens a menu of the remaining actions.

## Avoid

Actions people use rarely, or can’t guess from the symbol. Give those a title.
