## When to use

- Secondary and rare actions that would crowd the toolbar.
- Actions on a row or a card.

## Best practices

- Items are verbs in Title Case without articles: “Duplicate”, “Move to Folder…”.
- Group related items with separators and optional section labels. Keep submenus to one level.
- Destructive items use the destructive style, sit last, and still confirm.
- Show each item’s keyboard shortcut at its trailing edge.
- Hide items that can never apply here; disable the ones that can’t apply right now.

## Avoid

- Choosing a value. Use a [pop-up button](/design/qig/pop-up-buttons).
- Hiding the main action of a screen in a menu.
