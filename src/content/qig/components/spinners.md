## When to use

- Inside a button while it saves.
- In a row or panel that is refreshing.

## Best practices

Pair the spinner with words when it lasts: “Saving…”.

## Avoid

A full-screen spinner for content that could load progressively. Use [skeletons](/design/qig/skeletons).
