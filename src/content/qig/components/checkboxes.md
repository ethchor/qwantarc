## When to use

- Choosing several options.
- Including something in a form that is saved later.

## Best practices

- The label is clickable too.
- Labels say what “on” means: “Email a copy to me”.
- Use the mixed state for a parent whose children are partly selected.

## Avoid

Settings that take effect immediately. Use a [switch](/design/qig/switches).
