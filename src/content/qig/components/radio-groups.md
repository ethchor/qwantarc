## When to use

Two to five options where seeing all of them helps people choose.

## Best practices

- One option is always selected; choose a sensible default.
- Stack options vertically unless there are two short ones.

## Avoid

- Switching a view. Use a [segmented control](/design/qig/segmented-controls).
- Long lists. Use a [pop-up button](/design/qig/pop-up-buttons).
