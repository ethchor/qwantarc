## When to use

Codes and names in search bars and entry tables, where the typed text is valid on its own.

## Best practices

- Suggestions appear after the first characters and are reachable with the keyboard (up, down, Return).
- Return with no suggestion highlighted submits what was typed.

## Avoid

Choosing one record from a fixed set. Use a [combobox](/design/qig/comboboxes).
