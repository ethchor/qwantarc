## When to use

Approximate values where the range matters more than precision, such as volume, size or intensity.

## Best practices

- Always show the current value, and let people type it when precision matters.
- Label the ends or the unit.

## Avoid

Exact numbers people already know. Use a [text field](/design/qig/text-fields) or a [stepper](/design/qig/steppers).
