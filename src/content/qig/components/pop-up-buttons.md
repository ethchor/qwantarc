## When to use

Choosing one value from a list of about six to thirty options.

## Best practices

- The closed control always shows the current value, or a placeholder such as “Choose a group”.
- Options are nouns, sorted in a meaningful order: by frequency, size or alphabetically.
- On phones, open the system picker.

## Avoid

- Two to five options that fit on screen. Use a [segmented control](/design/qig/segmented-controls) or a [radio group](/design/qig/radio-groups).
- Long lists people search. Use a [combobox](/design/qig/comboboxes).
