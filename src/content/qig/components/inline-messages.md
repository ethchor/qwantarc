## When to use

- Conditions that last while people work, such as being offline.
- Errors that block one section, shown beside that section.

## Best practices

A symbol, a short title and one sentence on what to do. Errors use the destructive style.

## Avoid

Momentary confirmations. Use a [toast](/design/qig/toasts).
