## When to use

- A currency or unit beside a number: $, kg, %.
- A country code before a phone number.
- A clear or scan button inside a field.

## Best practices

- Add-ons are part of the field’s surface; keep them short and quiet.
- Buttons inside a field have accessible labels.

## Avoid

Long text in an add-on. It belongs in the label.
