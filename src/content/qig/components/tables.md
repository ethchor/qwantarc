## When to use

Lists of records with several attributes each.

## Best practices

- Headings are short nouns. Numbers align to the end, with tabular figures.
- The identifying column comes first and stays narrow; the name follows.
- Row actions are quiet icon buttons at the end of the row, all in one style.
- Offer a compact density for long lists, and a version without column lines.
- Empty, loading and error states fill the table body, never a blank card ([R17](/design/qig/rules#r17)).

## Avoid

Records with only one or two attributes. A [grouped list](/design/qig/grouped-lists) reads better.
