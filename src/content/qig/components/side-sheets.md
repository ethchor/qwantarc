## When to use

Inspectors: a record’s details, filters or history while the list stays visible.

## Best practices

- Slides in from the trailing edge; Done or the close button dismiss it.
- Keep it narrower than half the window on wide screens.

## Avoid

Forms that need full attention. Use a centred [sheet](/design/qig/sheets).
