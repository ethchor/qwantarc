## When to use

- Statuses in rows: Paid, Pending, Cancelled.
- Counts on a control.

## Best practices

- Status colours follow their meaning: green for success, orange for warning, red for destructive, blue for information, gray for neutral.
- The word carries the meaning too, never colour alone.

## Avoid

Long text or actions. A badge is a label, not a button.
