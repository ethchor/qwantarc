## When to use

- Naming icon-only buttons.
- Showing the full text of something truncated.

## Best practices

Use the same words as the control’s accessible label, in Title Case for actions: “Add Item”.

## Avoid

- Essential information. Touch screens can’t hover.
- Sentences. Keep it to a few words.
